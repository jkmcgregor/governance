GRC & AI Governance Case Studies

Real-world scenarios demonstrating risk reduction, framework implementation, and process engineering.

Enterprise AI Risk Management Framework (NIST AI RMF) Implementation
AI Compliance & Governance

Designed and deployed an end-to-end AI governance architecture for evaluating third-party and internally built machine learning models prior to production release.

The Risk Challenge

Business units were adopting generative AI tools rapidly without centralized oversight, exposing the organization to data leakage, IP risk, and unvetted algorithmic bias.

The Solution

Created a standardized AI Model Intake process mapped to NIST AI RMF 1.0. Developed automated Python scripts to validate model cards, data provenance, and security controls.

Measurable Impact

Reduced AI system review time from 3 weeks to 48 hours while achieving 100% visibility over deployed organizational models.

SOC 2 & ISO 27001 Evidence Automation Pipeline
GRC Process Automation

Re-engineered legacy manual compliance testing procedures into an automated Continuous Controls Monitoring (CCM) routine.

The Risk Challenge

Audit preparation required hundreds of engineering hours every quarter to manually take screenshots, export CSVs, and reconcile access control lists.

The Solution

Built serverless integration workflows using Python and webhooks to query system APIs, check security configurations against policy baselines, and push evidence directly to audit repositories.

Measurable Impact

Eliminated 85% of manual evidence gathering labor and achieved zero audit non-conformities during annual surveillance audits.
